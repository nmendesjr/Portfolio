Sentiment

How the news covers the companies building AI.

Sentiment reads the RSS feeds of a fixed list of news outlets, picks out every story about the companies below, and scores each one as positive, neutral or negative. No social media, no aggregators — every story links back to the outlet that published it.

Sample data. The feeds can't be reached from this preview, so the numbers and headlines below are placeholders. The outlet list is real — deployed on Vercel, /api/sentiment reads those feeds live.

How it works

Collect

Every hour, read each outlet's RSS feed listed under Sources. Stories are deduplicated by link and headline.

Match

A story counts toward a company when its headline or summary names it or its products (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Starlink, Gemini, Alexa+, Copilot…). Only Google's Gemini work counts, and Amazon only counts for its AI products or when the headline is about Amazon and AI. SpaceX and xAI are tracked as one company. One story can count for several companies.

Score

The headline and the outlet's own summary are scored with a word list tuned for tech news (lawsuit, outage, breakthrough, layoffs…) with negation handling. Net tone is the share of positive stories minus the share of negative ones, −100 to +100.

Caveats

Word-list scoring misses sarcasm and context, and the result reflects this outlet list, not all media. Read it as a weather vane, not a poll.
