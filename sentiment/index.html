Sentiment

How the press and the public feel about the companies building AI.

Every hour, Sentiment reads news coverage from thousands of outlets and public conversation on Reddit, Hacker News and Bluesky, then scores each headline and post as positive, neutral or negative.

How it works

Press

Google News search results for each company over the selected window — thousands of outlets, deduplicated by headline. Each headline is scored on its own.

Public

Reddit posts, Hacker News comments and Bluesky posts that mention the company by name. Off-topic hits (e.g. “perplexity” as a plain word) are filtered out.

Scoring

A lexicon tuned for tech news (lawsuit, outage, breakthrough, layoffs…) with negation and intensity handling. Net sentiment is the share of positive items minus the share of negative ones, from −100 to +100.

Caveats

Keyword scoring misses sarcasm and context, and the public sources skew toward tech-savvy, English-speaking audiences. Read it as a weather vane, not a poll.
